Install, sign in, and start your activities

The D1 Virtual Lab works on a phone and on a computer. Your trainer gives you three things: the class code, your Student ID and your PIN (usually by email).

1Open the link

Open the lab link in Chrome (Android) or Safari (iPhone). The first time, use Wi-Fi if you can, so the whole app downloads to your phone.

2Install it on your phone

Installing puts a D1 Lab icon on your home screen, like a normal app. It also lets the lab work without a signal.

  1. Open the link in Chrome.
  2. Tap the menu (three dots, top right).
  3. Tap Add to Home screen. On some phones it says Install app.
  4. Tap Install or Add.
  1. Open the link in Safari (installing does not work from Chrome on iPhone).
  2. Tap the Share button: a square with an arrow pointing up.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add at the top right.

From now on, open the lab from the D1 Lab icon on your home screen.

3Sign in

Type exactly what your trainer gave you, then tap Sign in:

Class codefor example D1-2026
Student IDfor example S014
PIN4 digits, keep it private

You stay signed in on that phone. Tap your Student ID at the top right to sign out.

4Do your activities

  • Choose a Day. Each day has a Lesson, the activities and a self-check quiz.
  • An activity shows CLOSED until your trainer opens it in class.
  • Inside an activity, switch between Task (instructions and your answers) and the content tool: the Content Planner or the Post Composer.
  • Design your graphics in Canva at the exact size, download them, then upload them to the Post Composer. The live preview shows how the post will look.
  • Tap Check to see what is still missing: each item of the checklist turns green when it is complete. Your trainer grades the quality.
  • Tap Submit when you are done. Your trainer sees your work right away. You may submit again; the latest one counts.

5Using a computer

Open the same link in Chrome or Edge on any computer and sign in the same way. No installation is needed. On a computer the instructions and the content tool are side by side, which is the easiest way to plan and write long captions.

You can start on your phone and continue on a computer: your submitted work is saved on the class server. Work you have not submitted yet stays on the device where you typed it.
